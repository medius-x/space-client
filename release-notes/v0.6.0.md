# 0.6.0

- Clicking your own avatar opens the STATUS menu: Account settings rides the
  top row, below it sit your presence status and a submenu with Auto, Do not
  disturb, Online, Away and Invisible. The auto mode flips between Online and
  Away (idle) on its own; any manual pick pins the status until Auto is
  chosen again. Invisible shows you as offline to everyone while everything
  keeps working — you can chat, join voice rooms and take calls; voice-room
  membership stays visible (the Discord way). Your own avatar carries the
  status dot, and everyone's roster dot turns red for Do not disturb.
- Notifications now follow your status: Away (auto) keeps message toasts on
  screen until you return (no sound), a manual Away hides message toasts,
  and Do not disturb / Invisible silence message and call sounds while the
  incoming-call card still appears.
- Calling offline friends is allowed: the call button in the friends list
  and the roster's call action work regardless of the green/grey dot.
- Voice channels can now carry a member limit: a "2/5" counter beside the
  channel name shows how full the room is (tinting red at the cap), a full
  room refuses new joiners with a "room is full" toast, and lowering the
  limit never kicks anyone out. The limit is set when creating a voice
  channel or later in Server Settings → Channels → Voice, and the new
  "Join Full Rooms" permission lets chosen roles walk into full rooms.
- Message search now covers discussion threads: the "everywhere" scope walks
  the readable threads too, and a channel-scoped search also searches the
  threads started in that channel. A thread hit is labelled "#channel →
  origin snippet" and clicking it opens the thread right on the message.
- The NEW-messages divider now follows your reading: a channel with unreads
  opens with the divider at the top of the window; whatever stays visible for
  a moment and a half counts as read (the read marker moves there and the
  badge melts down gradually), and the divider jumps below the viewport to the
  next unread — repeating until you reach the end. The jump-down chevron
  dismisses it instantly, and live messages stack up under a divider you are
  reading towards.
- Switching servers and back no longer scrambles the chat position: a chat you
  left at the bottom reopens at the (possibly grown) bottom, and a chat you
  were reading mid-history reopens on the very message you were looking at,
  with the jump-to-bottom chevron back.
- A lone picture (and a video's preview card) now takes more room: it grows
  up to 1.5× the mosaic tile size in a wide window, then scales down with the
  window instead of staying frozen — singles and videos stay level with each
  other at every width. Every kind of media (single pictures, video cards,
  mosaic tiles) now stops shrinking at the same small floor and overflows
  instead of crumbling into unreadable squares.
- Video previews in chat obey the same size cap as pictures (a widescreen
  clip used to render nearly twice as wide), the preview card no longer
  reacts to window resizes, and the play triangle sits dead-center in its
  circle. A long file name no longer stretches the card wider than its
  preview — the name trims to the frame with an ellipsis while the extension
  (".mp4" and friends) stays readable at the end.
- Elongated pictures (wide strips, tall strips) no longer shrink to a
  thumbnail in chat: the single-image preview fits the size cap on each side
  independently instead of being bounded by the image's smaller side — a
  wide strip shows at full preview width, small square pictures still stay
  exactly their natural size.
- Video cards now align exactly with the picture tiles next to them (the
  card's outer edge is the same width), and the play button is a translucent
  blurple circle — video attachments are easier to tell from pictures at a
  glance.
- Replying to a message with a picture now shows the picture: the quote card
  carries a small square preview of the original's first image beside the text
  snippet (freshly linked on every load, so it never goes stale; quotes inside
  forwarded messages stay text-only).
- The screen-share picker is a bit wider, its source previews shrank to sit
  two per row instead of one, and the quality step's buttons and inputs grew
  to match the wider card.
- Long lists no longer slide their whole column: the channel panel keeps its
  server header pinned while the channel list scrolls, and the server rail
  keeps the home button on top and the add-server button at the bottom while
  only the server icons scroll.
- Direct conversations now show a magnifier button in the header: it opens
  the conversation search (Ctrl+F still works too), which was previously
  reachable only through the keyboard shortcut. The header's divider also
  lines up with the search panel's divider again — it used to sit a pixel
  or two lower in DMs.
- Downloaded log archives (client and server) land in their own
  downloads/SPACE/logs subfolder instead of the root, and a button
  appears next to the download buttons that opens the folder with the
  latest archive selected in Explorer.
- The screen-share button in voice panels now uses the monitor-with-arrow
  glyph, matching the common screen-sharing symbol.
- Hovering a video in chat brightens and slightly grows its play button, and
  the photo viewer's cursors are the right way around now: the hand sits on
  the photo itself, the arrow on the dark space around it.
- The members screen no longer shows the server owner's role twice (a
  localized "Owner" row followed by a raw "OWNER" chip).
